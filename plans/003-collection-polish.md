## Verification

- Focused collection and SQLite checks: 63 passing cases.
- Advanced controls: passing tests cover condition state, AND/OR, reordering, multi-sort, combined reset and column visibility/width/order/pinning persistence, including six pinned columns. Legacy board/read/provider URL values are asserted in the real advanced controls.
- The initial integration test caught a render loop; the table root now subscribes to no state while child controls subscribe to their own slices. Persistence subscribes to the external table store after loading saved settings.
- Full-suite, final code-review and isolated-build results are recorded in `code-review.md` when complete.
- **Not verified:** browser geometry, zoom, rendered contrast, safe areas, RTL layout and animation frame timing. No browser or live application request was made.

## Component fixture report

`/dev/job-card` imports the real JobCard into 24 static scenarios: varied title lengths/scripts, zero/one/15/150 locations, 320px/squeezed/wide containers, read, selection, disabled, pending, active and historical states. Its provider boundary prevents queries or app-visit refreshes; links and actions remain inside the fixture. Loading/error scenarios were excluded because JobCard accepts no such state. No scenario is reported as an observed visual finding. The page remains available for the user's own observation.

## Verdict

Approve for inspected source and DOM behavior after the recorded corrections. Browser rendering remains **Not verified**; this is not visual approval. Final Standards/Spec review remains tracked separately.
